👋 Welcome to Portfolio Manager

        
      


      

        
        
      


      

      
      

        
You're exploring the demo. Here's what you're
          looking at and what you can do:


        

          1
          

            
Sample Assets

            
The demo ships with a few assets
              — like AAPL, VYM, TLT and MSFT — each with a price, type and dividend
              yield. You can also add your own personal assets (shown in []) — these
              are demo-only and not saved.

          

        


        

          2
          

            
Sample Providers &
              Accounts

            
Two providers
              (Revolut and Trading 212) hold sample accounts — a checking account, a savings account and an asset account.

          

        


        

          3
          

            
How it's connected

            
Holdings live inside an
              asset account and reference assets. Goals link to accounts,
              and the Dashboard & My Portfolio roll all of it up into charts and totals.

          

        


        

          4
          

            
What you can do

            
Everything is editable — add providers, accounts, holdings and
              goals to explore the app freely. Changes are local only and are not
                saved.

          

        

      


      
      

        
You're all set to start tracking your wealth. Here's how to get going:


        

          1
          

            
Add a Provider

            
Head to the My Accounts menu and
              create your first provider — a bank,
              broker or other institution that holds your money.

          

        


        

          2
          

            
Add an Account

            
Inside that provider, create an account — for example an asset account to hold your investments.

          

        


        

          3
          

            
Unlock Goals

            
Your Goals will be unlocked once
              you've created at least 1 account.

          

        


        

          4
          

            
Unlock My Portfolio,
              Assets & Dividends

            
Once you create an asset account,
              the My Portfolio, Assets and Dividends menus
              unlock — letting you add Holdings / Assets to your portfolio on any existing asset account.

          

        


        

          5
          

            
Track Personal Assets

            
On the Assets page, click + New
                Personal Asset to track something that isn't a platform security (e.g. a private holding or a
              custom item). Personal assets are private to your account and their names are shown in [].

          

        

      

      


      

        

          
Your data is private to you, and you can look back at how your dashboard looked on any past day.


          

            1
            

              
Your data is private

              
Everything you add — providers, accounts, holdings, assets and goals — is stored
                only under your account. No other user can see it, and guests only ever see the
                demo data. Press H to hide the values on screen from people nearby.

            

          


          

            2
            

              
Time Travel snapshots

              
On the Dashboard, the
                🕰 Time Travel button lets you save a snapshot of today's dashboard and view how it
                looked on any past day. Snapshots are private to you.

            

          


          

            3
            

              
One snapshot per day

              
You keep at most one snapshot per day. Saving again on the same
                day replaces that day's snapshot. You can delete any snapshot you no
                longer need.
